# <small>Bi-Monthly Challenge for Fall 2025:</small> Preptember & Hacktoberfest!

<LeadText>

This two-month challenge combines preparation and participation in open source.

</LeadText>

<LeadText>

**September (Preptember)**: Focus on helping maintainers polish their repositories and contributors gain confidence with our structured 4-week approach using our [repository checklist](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide#repository-checklist) and building skills through guided practice.

</LeadText>

<LeadText>

We have a [Virtual Coffee Preptember repository](https://github.com/Virtual-Coffee/vc-preptember) for you to add yourself as a Preptember participant and to list repositories that pass our repository checklist to be recommended for Hacktoberfest!

</LeadText>

<LeadText>

**October (Hacktoberfest)**: Put your preparation into action by contributing to open source projects during the official Hacktoberfest event.

</LeadText>

## Theme

**September**: Prep + Confidence Building - Get ready for open source!

**October**: Hacktoberfest - Participate in open source, learn, and have fun!

## Challenge

### September: Preptember

**Goal**: Help maintainers polish repos and contributors gain confidence through a structured 4-week approach.

### October: Hacktoberfest

**Goal**: Complete six meaningful contributions during October. We have two tracks:

- **Maintainers**: Provide repositories with "`hacktoberfest`" topics and issues labeled "`hacktoberfest`"
- **Contributors**: Find repositories and solve issues to achieve six approved pull requests

## Community Issue

**Preptember Accountability Issue** → members comment weekly with their wins + GIFs to celebrate progress and keep each other motivated.

## Maintainers

Preparing your open-source project for contributions is one of the best ways to create a community of contributors that lasts beyond Hacktoberfest. Your repository should have a clear path for communication, a good explanation of how to run the project, and well-written issues. [Our guide](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide) will help you prepare your project to be added to the list of Virtual Coffee-endorsed projects for our Hacktoberfest Initiative.

## Contributors

The first step in becoming a good contributor is navigating an open-source repository and understanding the time and effort maintainers put into ensuring their projects are welcoming to contributors. As we move into Hacktoberfest season, we can support maintainers in their efforts to onboard new contributors. To complete this monthly challenge, find an open-source repository you'd like to contribute to and evaluate it based on our [repository checklist](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide#repository-checklist). If it doesn't meet our checklist requirements, add an issue or multiple issues, asking the maintainers to update the repository based on your feedback.

## How to Participate

### September: Preptember Participation

<LeadText>

Please take a look at our [Repository Checklist](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide#repository-checklist). This guide contains everything we look for in a good open-source project.

</LeadText>

- If you are new to open source, we have a [Virtual Coffee Preptember repository](https://github.com/Virtual-Coffee/vc-preptember) for you to practice open source by adding yourself as Preptember participant.
- If you are participating in this month's challenge as a contributor, consider the items in our [repository checklist](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide) as you look at some possible open-source repositories to contribute to. If the repositories pass the checklist, add them to the repositories list in the [Virtual Coffee Preptember repository](https://github.com/Virtual-Coffee/vc-preptember).
- If you are participating as an open-source maintainer, use [our checklist](/resources/developer-resources/open-source/maintainer-guide) as a guide to get your repository ready for contributions. Then add your project repository to the repository list in the [Virtual Coffee Preptember repository](https://github.com/Virtual-Coffee/vc-preptember).

### Weekly Schedule

<h4 className="mt-3">Week 1: Repo Health Check</h4>

**Focus**: Use the maintainer checklist to review repos (README, LICENSE, CONTRIBUTING, issue/PR templates).

- Maintainers and contributors will review repositories using our checklist
- Members will share 1 thing they fixed or improved

#### Week 2: Good First Issues

**Focus**: Create/label beginner-friendly issues in VC or personal repos.

- Maintainers create and label beginner-friendly issues
- Contributors practice finding and commenting on approachable issues

#### Week 3: Docs & Guides

**Focus**: Add or update `CONTRIBUTING.md`, onboarding notes, or a setup guide.

- Emphasis on non-code contributions (typos, screenshots, clarifications)
- Improve documentation and contribution guidelines

#### Week 4: Promote Your Project!

- Maintainers share their projects in our [Virtual Coffee Preptember repository](https://github.com/Virtual-Coffee/vc-preptember) (there will be a 2025 issue)
- Share a pitch, video, etc. in `#hacktoberfest` channel in Slack

### Synchronous check-ins

### Weekly check-ins

1. **Maintainers** — Review your `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` files.

   - If the repository hasn't met the checklist requirements, post the repository and a link to the issue/issues you've added to prepare the repository for Hacktoberfest in the `#monthly-challenge` channel in Slack.

## October: Hacktoberfest Participation

<LeadText>

After preparing in September, it's time to participate in the official Hacktoberfest!

</LeadText>

### Before Starting Hacktoberfest

**Contributors**

- Sign up on the [Hacktoberfest official website](https://hacktoberfest.com/).
- Check out repositories with the "`hacktoberfest`" topic and issues on GitHub with the "`hacktoberfest`" label.

**Maintainers**

Make sure you include "`hacktoberfest`" as a label in your project's topics section (found in the "About" section on your repository) or on the issues you want to opt-in to the event. That's how you opt-in to Hacktoberfest.

[Check out all of the Hacktoberfest guidelines on the official website](https://hacktoberfest.com/participation#maintainers).

### October Weekly Check-ins

Let's start the week with a check-in. You can find the check-ins in the `#monthly-challenge` channel in Slack. What are your goals for Hacktoberfest for the week? What support do you need? Do you plan on spending time in the `#co-working-room`, talking to a mentee, or answering questions in Slack as a maintainer?

### Completing Hacktoberfest

To complete the Hacktoberfest challenge, you need to complete six meaningful contributions during October.

Remember, VC is here to support you during Hacktoberfest but is not an official event partner. To complete the official Hacktoberfest, you must have six (6) pull requests (PRs) accepted.

### Virtual Coffee Approved Repositories for Hacktoberfest

Check back in October for our curated list of Virtual Coffee approved repositories that will be ready for Hacktoberfest contributions!

### Share Your Progress!

Share your progress in the `#monthly-challenge` channel in Slack. While no other platform is imposed, sharing on social media for more reach can be a good idea, but only if you are comfortable doing so. You can share on social media, in a personal blog, in a post on [DEV.to](https://dev.to/), you get the idea!

Sharing every time some work is added is a good idea. Small progress is still progress. There is no need to write a detailed blog post. A social media post can be enough. For example, "_Today, I updated my README to give instructions on how to run my project._"

### What if I need help?

You can ask questions in the `#help-and-pairing` channel in Slack, ask for ideas in the `#open-source` channel, or join the `#co-working-room`. Asking for help is part of the process!

And remember, we're always here to help. ❤️
