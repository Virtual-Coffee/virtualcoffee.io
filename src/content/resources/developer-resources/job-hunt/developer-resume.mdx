Your resume is often the first thing a recruiter or hiring manager sees, and it usually gets only a quick scan. A clear, focused developer resume makes it easy for them to see what you've built and why you're a fit for the role.

On this page you'll find free guides for writing a developer resume, open-source builders and templates, and tools to help you tailor your resume to each job. Every resource listed here is free to use.

## Guides: What to Put on Your Resume

- [Practical guide to writing FAANG-ready software engineer resumes](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/) - Tech Interview Handbook's open-source guide covering ATS-friendly layout, writing strong bullet points, choosing keywords, and getting your resume reviewed.
- [Resume Writing Guide](https://books.codepath.org/student-handbook/artifacts/student-resume-guide) - CodePath's guide for students and early-career developers on resume format, content, getting feedback, and using your resume at career fairs and in online applications.
- [Using your GitHub profile to enhance your resume](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/using-your-github-profile-to-enhance-your-resume) - GitHub Docs walks you through your bio, profile README, and pinned projects so the GitHub link on your resume supports your application.

## Build It: Free and Open-Source Resume Builders and Templates

- [Reactive Resume](https://rxresu.me/) - Free, open-source resume builder with privacy in mind. It offers many templates and exports to PDF.
- [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org/) - An open standard for writing your resume as a JSON file. You keep one source of truth and render it with community themes.
- [RenderCV](https://github.com/rendercv/rendercv) - Open-source tool that turns a YAML file into a clean PDF resume, so you can keep your resume in version control like your code.
- [Jake's Resume](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs) - A popular single-page LaTeX template for software engineers that you can edit in Overleaf.
- [Awesome CV](https://github.com/posquit0/Awesome-CV) - Open-source LaTeX template for resumes and cover letters.

## Tailor It: Match Your Resume to Each Job

- [SmartJobTools ATS Resume Checker](https://smartjobtools.blogspot.com/p/resume-analyzer.html) - Free, no sign-up. Upload your resume as a PDF or DOCX and paste the job description to get a score, the keywords you're missing, and a prioritized list of fixes.

## Quick Tips

- Lead with impact. Describe what you built or improved and the result, using numbers when you can.
- Tailor each application. Mirror the job description's language for skills you actually have, and never add skills or experience you don't have.
- Link your work. Add your GitHub profile, portfolio, or live projects so reviewers can see your code.
- Keep the layout simple. A single-column layout with standard section headings is easier for both people and applicant tracking systems to read.
- Ask for feedback. Share your resume with a peer or a mentor before you send it out.
